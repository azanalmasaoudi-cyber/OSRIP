# Governorate evidence pack of November 2026: staged imports

Generated by `backend/scripts/stage_governorate_pack.py` from `OSRIP_Oman_11_Governorates_63_Wilayats_Official_Data_2026.xlsx`, mapped by `backend/app/services/imports/governorate_pack.py`. The pack is research evidence: every figure mapped here was compared with the NCSI publication it cites, and nothing here is published. Each import is validated and waits for a data manager's approval in Data Management.

Rules kept: full-year 2025 and the 2026 years to date are separate periods (2026 is never called a full year); grouped regions (Al Batinah with Adh Dhahirah, both Sharqiyah, the 'other' areas, Al Batinah's combined fishing) stay groups and are never shared out among governorates or wilayats; the count of enterprises of all economic activities is business context and is not imported as trade; the only wilayat figures are the ministry's commercial records, which it publishes by wilayat; no statistic of the pack is given to a wilayat.

## Staging

The honey rows need migration 0035 (the AGRI_PROD_HONEY indicator and the kg unit). After the migrations are applied, a data manager stages the imports with `backend/.venv/Scripts/python.exe backend/scripts/stage_governorate_pack.py docs/data-imports/2026-11-governorates/OSRIP_Oman_11_Governorates_63_Wilayats_Official_Data_2026.xlsx --stage --as USERNAME` (from the repository folder), then reviews, approves and publishes them in Data Management. `--reject` withdraws them while they are unpublished.
